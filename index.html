Manel Slokom

Lecturer & Researcher, Hotelschool The Hague

I am a researcher working on responsible AI, privacy-preserving machine learning, synthetic data, and recommender systems. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from Delft University of Technology. My research connects data privacy, fairness, and practical system design across official statistics, news and entertainment recommendation, and applied data governance. I currently teach Data Analytics and Research at Hotelschool The Hague, after postdoctoral research at VU Amsterdam and CWI, and a methodologist role at Statistics Netherlands (CBS).
